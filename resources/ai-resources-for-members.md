# AI Resources for Members

Curated by AI Society Vienna. Everything here is free (or free to audit) unless noted. All links were checked in October 2026 - if one breaks or you know something better, tell the board.

> **New here? Start with the "Your first four weeks" path below**, then pick the track that fits you.

---

## Your first four weeks

| Week | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/) - the first chapters, or [AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations](https://academy.claude.com/) | 3-4 h |
| 2 | [Interactive prompt engineering tutorial](https://github.com/anthropics/prompt-eng-interactive-tutorial) - get much better results from any chatbot | 3 h |
| 3 | [Kaggle Learn: Intro to Machine Learning](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) - your first model, in the browser | 3 h |
| 4 | Pick a track below: **Business & society**, **Build with AI** or **Machine learning** | - |

## 1. Start here - no coding needed

- **[Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/)** - University of Helsinki. The classic introduction for everyone, with a certificate. Also available [in German](https://www.elementsofai.de/).
- **[AI For Everyone](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-for-everyone)** - Andrew Ng. What AI can and cannot do, and how companies use it. Built for business people. Can usually be audited for free on Coursera.
- **[Generative AI for Everyone](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/generative-ai-for-everyone/)** - Andrew Ng. How ChatGPT-style models work and where they create value.
- **[Anthropic Academy](https://academy.claude.com/)** - free courses on AI fluency: working with AI effectively, its capabilities and limits.
- **[The Future of AI](https://bluedot.org/)** - BlueDot Impact. Self-paced, about 2 hours, on where AI is heading and why it matters.
- **[Neural networks, visually](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi)** - 3Blue1Brown. The best visual intuition for how neural networks and LLMs work.

## 2. Build with AI - prompting, tools and agents

- **[Prompt engineering tutorial](https://github.com/anthropics/prompt-eng-interactive-tutorial)** - interactive, step by step, with exercises.
- **[Prompt engineering guide (Claude docs)](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview)** - practical techniques that work across models.
- **[Prompting Guide](https://www.promptingguide.ai/)** - an open overview of prompting techniques, agents and RAG.
- **[OpenAI Cookbook](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook)** - code examples for building with the API.
- **[DeepLearning.AI short courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses)** - 1-2 hour courses on agents, RAG, fine-tuning and more, often taught with the companies behind the tools.
- **[Hugging Face Agents Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course)** - build AI agents, with a certificate.

## 3. Learn machine learning properly - some coding

- **[Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn)** - short hands-on courses: Python, pandas, machine learning, deep learning. Runs in the browser.
- **[Google Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course)** - a fast, practical introduction.
- **[Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/machine-learning-introduction)** - Andrew Ng and Stanford. The standard beginner course.
- **[Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/)** - fast.ai. Top-down: build working models first, theory later.
- **[MIT 6.S191 Introduction to Deep Learning](https://introtodeeplearning.com/)** - MIT's intro course with lectures and labs.
- **[Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)** - Andrej Karpathy. Build a GPT from scratch. Demanding and excellent.
- **[Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course)** - transformers, fine-tuning and the Hugging Face ecosystem.
- **[LLM Course roadmap](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course)** - a structured path through LLM fundamentals, engineering and research.

## 4. Go deeper - university level

- Stanford lecture courses: **[CS229 Machine Learning](https://cs229.stanford.edu/)**, **[CS231n Computer Vision](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/)**, **[CS224n NLP with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)**
- Free textbooks:
  - **[Understanding Deep Learning](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/)** - Simon Prince. Modern, clear, with notebooks.
  - **[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/)** - interactive book with runnable code.
  - **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** - Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville. The classic reference.

## 5. See how it works - interactive explainers

- **[Transformer Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/transformer-explainer/)** - run a small GPT in your browser and watch every step.
- **[LLM Visualization](https://bbycroft.net/llm)** - a 3D walk through a language model.
- **[MLU-Explain](https://mlu-explain.github.io/)** - visual essays on core ML concepts.

## 6. Free tools for students

- **[GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)** - free developer tools, including GitHub Copilot for verified students.
- **[Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/)** - Python notebooks in the browser, with free GPU time.
- **[Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions)** - datasets, notebooks and competitions. A great way to build a portfolio.
- **[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/learn)** - open models, datasets and demos.
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com/)** and **[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/)** - run open models locally on your laptop.
- **[Arena](https://arena.ai/)** - compare chatbots side by side and see the community leaderboard.
- **[Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/)** - independent comparisons of model quality, speed and price.

## 7. AI, business and society

- **[Wharton AI & Analytics Initiative](https://ai-analytics.wharton.upenn.edu/)** - home of our partner club AI @ Penn. Public reports, events and [on-demand tutorials](https://ai-analytics.wharton.upenn.edu/for-students/on-demand-learning/) (some modules are Penn-only).
- **[Knowledge at Wharton: AI](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/category/technology/ai/)** - articles and podcasts on AI in business and finance.
- **[One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/)** - Ethan Mollick (Wharton). The best newsletter on using AI at work and in education.
- **[The Road to Accountable AI](https://accountableai.net/)** - Wharton's Kevin Werbach on AI governance.
- **[AI Index Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index)** - Stanford HAI. The yearly data report on the state of AI.
- **[Our World in Data: AI](https://ourworldindata.org/artificial-intelligence)** - charts on AI progress, investment and adoption.
- **[EU AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)** - the full text and summaries of the EU AI Act.
- **[KI-Servicestelle (RTR)](https://www.rtr.at/rtr/service/ki-servicestelle/ki-servicestelle.de.html)** - Austria's official AI service desk, with guidance on the AI Act and AI literacy (in German).

## 8. Stay up to date

- **[The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/)** - weekly AI news from DeepLearning.AI.
- **[Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/)** - Jack Clark's weekly newsletter on research and policy.
- **[Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/)** - podcast and newsletter for people building with AI.
- **[Dwarkesh Podcast](https://www.dwarkesh.com/)** - long interviews with leading researchers and founders.
- **[Hugging Face Daily Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers)** - the day's most discussed research papers.
- **[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/)** - where AI research is published first.
- Research blogs: **[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research)**, **[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/research/)**

## 9. AI in Vienna

- **[WU Institute for Data, Process and Knowledge Management](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/dpkm)** - AI research at WU, with open talks on campus and thesis topics.
- **[Vienna Deep Learning Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/vienna-deep-learning-meetup/)** - Vienna's long-running meetup where research meets industry.
- **[AI Tinkerers Vienna](https://vienna.aitinkerers.org/)** - demos and talks by people building with AI.
- **[AI Austria](https://aiaustria.com/)** - non-profit network for AI in Austria, with community events.
